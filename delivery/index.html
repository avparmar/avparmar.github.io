    Choose your starting site

    Your factory takes two blocks. Click any two open blocks of your home ring, the unshaded ones, to put it there; R or a right-click turns it between side by side and one above the other. The other players are choosing at the same time; you'll see their sites when the match starts.

    Business

    Producers (warehouses, violet, with an arrow out) sell to you; consumers (shops, crimson, with an arrow in) buy from you. Each shows its good on its roof, a gauge of its stock or demand, and its price on its sign: green is a good deal for you, red a poor one. Trucks are painted in their factory's colour, with their load showing in the bed, and a faint line in that colour shows the road each has left to drive. Point at a truck, a business or a factory to pick out the routes through it, with their stops numbered.

    Click a producer to add a buy, a consumer to add a sell. Set limits on each stop if you want them; the estimate is what the order would get at today's prices. Enter sends, R repeats the picked or first idle truck's last order, Esc clears the order, F switches the focus view, Tab goes back to the floor.
