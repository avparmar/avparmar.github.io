Signature Required Redux

Producers (warehouses, a blue badge with an arrow out) sell to you; consumers (shops, a purple badge with an arrow in) buy from you. Each shows its good on its roof, a gauge of its stock or demand, and its price on its sign: green is a good deal for you, red a poor one. Trucks wear their factory's colour on the cab and their load's on the box.

Output per minute

Raws come in at the six docks on the floor's edge: click an IN port to pick what it unloads. Goods leave through OUT ports. Click a truck in the strip below to give it an order from here. Rates count the last minute of game time.

Space pauses, S steps one tick, Tab shows the city. Belts are free. Selling or erasing a module pays back half its price. Undo covers belts, moves and settings, but stops at the last module bought or sold.

Business

Click a producer to add a buy, a consumer to add a sell. Set limits on each stop if you want them; the estimate is what the order would get at today's prices. Enter sends, R repeats the picked or first idle truck's last order, Esc clears the order, F switches the focus view, Tab goes back to the floor.
